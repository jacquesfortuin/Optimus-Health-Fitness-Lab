# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 2026

## Overview

Optimus Health is an offline-first health and fitness application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information remains under your control.

All health telemetry, kinematic data, activity logs, workout records, and personal entries are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to our servers or shared with third parties.

### Key Privacy Principles

* **100% Offline:** All data remains on your physical device.
* **No Cloud Storage:** We do not operate servers that receive or store your personal data.
* **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising SDKs, or data brokers.
* **Local Device Storage:** Data is stored within your device's application sandbox and protected by your device's security mechanisms.

## Information We Collect

Optimus Health does not collect, transmit, receive, process, or store personal information on external servers.

Any information entered into the application, including fitness activities, health metrics, notes, goals, and training data, is stored exclusively on your device.

## Data Retention

Because Optimus Health does not collect or store user data on external servers, we do not retain user data outside of your device.

All application data remains on your device until one of the following occurs:

* You delete specific records within the application.
* You clear the application's data through your device settings.
* You uninstall the application from your device.

Once removed from the device, the data is no longer accessible to Optimus Health.

## Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to us, there is no need to submit a data deletion request.

You can delete your data at any time by:

1. Deleting records within the application (if supported).
2. Clearing the application's storage through your device settings.
3. Uninstalling the application from your device.

Since we do not maintain servers containing user information, we cannot access, retrieve, modify, or delete data on behalf of users.

## Third-Party Services

Optimus Health does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or cloud storage providers that receive your personal data.

## Children's Privacy

Optimus Health does not knowingly collect personal information from children because no user data is transmitted to or stored by us.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

## Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Optimus Health support team using the contact information provided on the application's store listing.
